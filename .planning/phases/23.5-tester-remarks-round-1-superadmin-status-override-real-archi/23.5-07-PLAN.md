`infra/promote.sh` — the whole promotion, in the one order that is safe:
```
1  resolve digests      tags are MUTABLE; the digest is the release identity
1b verify in target     "same digest" is checked, not assumed
2  idle gate            BEFORE anything is applied
3a nestor-migrate  3b tribunal-migrate
4  nestor-api
5  frontend             a BUILD, not a promotion (Vite inlines VITE_* at build time)
6  tribunal-api
7  idle gate AGAIN      steps 3-6 take minutes

9  smoke                10 write the tags back
```
