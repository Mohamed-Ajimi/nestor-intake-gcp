<objective>
Remark 2, mechanisms 3 and 5, behind the same master (D-23.5-04): a claim's citation number
comes from its own provider's URL rather than from the lowest uuid among everything linked to
it, the numbered entries carry `resolved_url` for the renderer, and the fact ledger stops
offering the writer claims that cannot be numbered.

Purpose: 232 of 427 sourced claims in run 7784e71c anchored to a host that is not their own
domain, and 108 anchors were stripped out of the delivered report because 159 claims — mostly
`distiller_fallback` — have no `claim_source` row at all and were still offered to the writer.

Output: a second, additive deterministic ordering in `numbering.py` (the pinned one untouched),
an opt-in filter on `build_ledger`, four lines of wiring in `_load_citation_context`, and an
offline proof that reproduces the stripped anchors with the flags off and drives them to zero
with the flags on.

**This is the sensitive plan's second half.** Nothing under `runs/`, `audit/` or `alembic/` is
touched; in `pipeline/tribunal/pipeline.py` ONLY `_load_citation_context` changes.
