grounding redirect can be stored ALONGSIDE the publisher URL it resolves to, plus tribunal alembic

engine gate. No resolver and no wiring here — plan 15.4-09 owns those.

  - `resolved_url` — TEXT, nullable. The publisher URL a `vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com`

  - `resolution_status` — TEXT, nullable. Exactly three meanings, stated in the migration docstring:

    `'resolved'` = a 302 Location was read and stored in `resolved_url`;
    `'unresolved'` = resolution was attempted and did not produce a usable http(s) target.
    No CHECK constraint and no enum type — the codebase's idiom for this class of column is a
    clamped-in-Python TEXT column (see `claim.certainty` in 0013), and a CHECK here would make a
    resolver bug fail an insert inside a paid run.
