Fix the defect that put a false statement in a client report: `_parse_distiller_response` discarded
278 well-formed claims because the model wrote the literal five-character string `<TAB>` instead of
U+0009 — and the prompt itself uses `<TAB>` as a placeholder describing the separator.

being a coin flip, and the prompt stops handing the model a token it can copy back as data.

dedicated guard that makes a future prompt regression go red, and the replay proof against V-01's
real blobs.
