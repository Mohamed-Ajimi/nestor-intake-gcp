## What Was Built

### Task 1 — the two tolerant helpers (commit `6980fda`)

**`research_division.py`** — new module-private `_fact_count_label(result) -> str`
at line 1050, with `_UNKNOWN_FACTS = "an unknown number of facts"` at 1047.
Returns `f"{n} facts"` when `result.get("facts")` is a sized collection, and the
unknown wording for every other shape. `str`/`bytes` are excluded **explicitly**
rather than left to `len`: `len("no results")` is 10, and `"10 facts"` would be a
count invented out of a provider's prose — worse than the vanished row it
replaces. Anything `len` refuses (`None`, an `int`, an object) reaches the same
wording via the helper's own `except`. The helper never raises.

The `agent_done` site (now line 1239) reads
`f"{_fact_count_label(result)} · {provider}"`. **The lambda is still a lambda and
still the only `build=` argument to `emit_safe`** — the evaluation stays inside
the protected region. The comment that justified the subscript is rewritten: it
now states that the count is resolved tolerantly *and* that the honest-unknown
rule is preserved, and says in as many words that the old comment's reason was
right while its mechanism was not.

**`own_researcher.py`** — new module-private `_skipped_label(block, kept) -> str`
at line 368, with `_UNKNOWN_SKIPPED = "skipped: unknown"` at 365. Returns
`f"{raw - kept} skipped"` when `_raw_fact_count` succeeds, `"skipped: unknown"`
when it raises. **`_raw_fact_count` itself is unchanged and still raises** — its
contract is right and other callers rely on it; the guard is at the one caller
that would rather print "unknown" than print nothing. `len(facts)` and
`len(citations)` are lengths of lists this module built, so those two counts
always render.

Both helpers carry a docstring stating (a) why the honest-unknown wording exists
(T-15.3-23 — the feed must not assert a number the run never established) and
(b) why the fix is here rather than in `emit_safe` (D-06 — the emitter caught
these correctly; the lambdas were the intolerant part), including an explicit
"never hoist `build()` above its `try`".
