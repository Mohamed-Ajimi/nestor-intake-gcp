`text` is resolved just above from `response.text`, falling back to

The ZERO-claims loop (steps.py:1578-1580):

    for fa in focus_area_labels:
        if facet_counts.get(fa, 0) == 0:
            log.warning("claim_distiller: focus area %r produced ZERO claims — unverified topic", fa)

`focus_area_labels` comes from the whole MISSION BRIEF, not from this call. On V-01 that made it
warn about `convenience` — a facet that was never in the distiller call at all, whose report parsed
its fact list successfully — right next to the real coffee zero.

`claim_distiller(provider_reports=…)` receives the fallback units built at steps.py:2022 as
`(name, {**result, "report": stripped})`, so each result dict still carries `_angle`, which is the
per-report focus area. That is the scoping key.

WHY WARNING AND NOT INFO (D-V01-6): stdlib logging in the pipeline is served by Python's
`lastResort` handler at WARNING and above. INFO and DEBUG do not exist in production. A loss
recorded below WARNING is a loss nobody is told about.
