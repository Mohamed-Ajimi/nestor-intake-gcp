<objective>
Give a provider one chance to fix its own fact list before its report is downgraded to prose
distillation — re-asking the SAME provider for ONLY the fact list, over its own report text, with a
corrective naming the deviation that was actually observed.

Purpose: on V-01 three of five gemini reports fell through to the distiller. Two had no
`FACTS_START`/`FACTS_END` block at all; one had a block whose every line was shifted by a literal
`STATEMENT` column. Plan 15.4-04 already rescues the second shape deterministically. This retry
covers the first, and catches whatever 15.4-04's normaliser does not.

Output: one cheap, capped, additive retry. Cost discipline is the constraint: no deep-research call
is re-issued — on Gemini that is a full re-run, and D-14 rejected it explicitly.
</objective>
