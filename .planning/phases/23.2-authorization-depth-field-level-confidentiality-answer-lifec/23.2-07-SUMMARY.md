## Seam 3 — the deliberate opposite of plan 08

`context_pack` filters admin-only keys **OUT** of the LLM input. Plan 08's
`structure_answers` deliberately keeps them **IN** its writable set, because it runs as an
operator. That difference is by design and is not a bug. Nothing from plan 08's rule was
imported here, and nothing here was "harmonised" with it. The two consume the same helper
(`admin_only_field_keys()`) for opposite purposes, which is exactly what D-23.2-02
intends: one derivation, many consumers, each choosing its own disposition.
