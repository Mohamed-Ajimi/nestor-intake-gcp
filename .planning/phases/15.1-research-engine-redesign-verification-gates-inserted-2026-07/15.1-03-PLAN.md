<objective>
Give the skeptic a fourth verdict word. Today the vocabulary is `support | refute |
insufficient`, so the KPAnG case came back as six "support" verdicts on true-but-obsolete
claims with the nuance stranded in a free-text reconciliation note (G-06). This plan is the
PRODUCER side: the tool schema, the system prompt, the parser, and the proof that adjudication
already does the right thing.

Purpose: making `superseded` a verdict forces the skeptic to choose it and makes it countable
by every downstream screen and bucket. G-07 requires the caveat text ("this pattern applied
until 1 April 2026") to be pipeline data, so the prompt must demand the date / what changed.

Output: `superseded` in the tool enum and prompt, a normalising parse boundary, and
`tests/test_superseded_verdict.py`.

**Deliberate non-change:** `pipeline/tribunal/adjudicate.py` is NOT edited. Only `"refute"` can
drop a claim, so a superseded claim survives by construction — exactly what G-07 wants. This
plan writes the regression test instead of a speculative edit.
</objective>
