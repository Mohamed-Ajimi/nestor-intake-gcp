## Known gaps carried out of Phase 15.1

Recorded here rather than quietly closed, so 15.2 inherits them with their context.

### 1. `verification_degraded_text` is emitted by the backend but rendered by nothing

G-10 requires that a degraded run states its degradation **in words**, at the top of the
verification report and in the feed's closing summary. Half of that is live, half is not:

- **Live:** the D15 feed's closing verify row (`pipeline._verify_closing_item`) leads with the full
  sentence — `VERIFICATION DEGRADED — N of M selected claims were never checked …` — and uses
  `status="failed"`, which `ResearchRunProgress.tsx` already renders with a red `AlertTriangle`.
- **Live:** `should_have_been_checked` and `verification_degraded` are funnel keys, and
  `VerificationReport.tsx` renders the funnel via `Object.entries(report.funnel)` — so they DO
  appear in the report, as raw-key labels.
- **Not live:** the API also returns `report["verification_degraded_text"]` (the full sentence) and
  `report["accounting"]` (the three buckets), and **no frontend component reads either**
  (`grep -rn "verification_degraded\|accounting" frontend/src/` → no binding). So the operator sees
  the degradation as numbers in the funnel row, not as the sentence at the top of the report that
  G-10 asks for.

Closing this is a frontend change (`VerificationReport.tsx`), deliberately outside this plan's
two-file scope. It belongs with 15.2's R6 work, which is already opening this surface to promote
the marker into a real terminal status.

### 2. `superseded_note` is a declared contract that is not persisted end-to-end

Plan 15.1-03 taught the skeptic to emit `superseded_note` (the G-07 caveat: what changed and from
when) and carries it in the parsed pipeline dict. Plan 15.1-06 declared it on the
`VerificationVerdictItem` pydantic model so it can cross the API boundary. **But nothing writes it
to storage and `_verdict_dto` does not emit it** — `_verdict_dto` was deliberately left untouched
per threat T-15.1-25, and 15.1-03 deliberately added no `verification_verdict` column.

So today the field declares the contract without carrying data. Wiring it end-to-end needs a
**storage decision** that this phase had no mandate to make:

- **Option A** — a new `superseded_note TEXT` column on `verification_verdict` (a real migration,
  `0012` in the tribunal line).
- **Option B** — a sub-key on the existing `reconciliation` field (no migration, but it overloads a
  field that already carries free text and would need a parse contract).

This belongs to whoever owns the 15.2 migration — the same person promoting `verification_degraded`
into a terminal status under R6, since 15.2's D14 "Disputed & changed" section is the intended
consumer of `superseded_note`.

   first** (recorded KEEP → gate DROP: a material claim that would ship unexamined), then the cheap
   one (recorded DROP → gate KEEP: money wasted, nothing hidden).
2. **`test_known_contradiction_pairs_cluster_together`** — runs the real clusterer over the full
   population and reports cluster count, singleton share (against the **92%** singleton baseline
   that let four flat contradictions ship), and, per pair, whether the two variants collided:

   | Pair | The contradiction that shipped |
   |---|---|
   | Aral | 16% vs 21% market share |
   | LUKOIL NL | 46 vs ~70/75 stations |
   | Zeeland | "sold to Carlyle" vs "bought by TotalEnergies" |
   | Gunvor vs Carlyle | conflicting acquirer |

   A probe that matches nothing prints `NOT FOUND IN FIXTURE` with its match counts — that is
   fixture drift, not a clustering failure.

**What a good/bad result means:** there is **NO threshold and NO pass/fail** (G-01, G-05). High
agreement means the live classifier reproduces the blind agents' judgment; low agreement means the
gate prompt needs work — but neither turns a build red. This was a deliberate design decision: the
456/424 labels came from blind LLM agents reading each claim, a production gate cannot hit them
exactly, and the honest response to a red build caused by classifier wobble is to weaken the check.
The enforcement is mechanical — `grep -c "assert"` on that file returns **0** (see § G-01 boundary
evidence above).

the agreement table and the four pair results verbatim; do not summarise them into a verdict.
