<objective>
Stop deleting the corroboration signal the pipeline already has (G-12). `claim_distiller`
knows which researcher produced each chunk and throws the name away; `_dedupe_claims` collapses
the same fact from three different researchers and throws the duplicates away. After this plan
every claim carries `found_by: [provider, …]`, and dedupe merges instead of discarding.

Purpose: corroboration prioritization (D9 / G-04 step 4) is impossible without this. Three
researchers independently confirming a fact currently looks identical to one researcher
asserting it alone.

Output: `found_by` on the claim dict, a merging `_dedupe_claims`, and the property test that
proves the merge changed nothing about the surviving claim list.
</objective>
