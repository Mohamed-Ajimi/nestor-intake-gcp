`tribunal/cloudbuild.test-gates.yaml` — the last gate config in the repo without an
`EXPECTED_FILES` assertion — now reconciles what `ls` collected against a pinned count, fails RED

## Deviations from Plan

### 1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Task 1's unscoped D-W5-15 grep contradicted Task 1's own action

- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** Task 1 acceptance required `grep -nE "[0-9]+ of (the )?13" tribunal/cloudbuild.test-gates.yaml`
  to return **NOTHING**. But the same task's action mandates the sentence *"…go green having run
  **12 of 13**"* and marks it **EXEMPT**. Satisfying the acceptance criterion literally would have
  required deleting the clearest line in the config — the exact "helpful deletion" the exemption
  exists to prevent.
- **Fix:** Applied the **SCOPED** check instead, which is the form given in `15.8-CONTEXT.md`'s
  D-W5-15 amendment, in Task 2's own acceptance criteria, and in the executor brief. Asserted **both
  directions**:
  - measurement ABSENT: `grep -nEi "([0-9]+|nine|ten|eleven) of (the )?13" … | grep -Ei "import|pipeline|regression"` → no match (exit 1) ✅
  - arithmetic PRESENT: `grep -n "12 of 13"` → 2 lines (62, 64) ✅
- **Why this reading:** `15.8-CONTEXT.md` is THE AUTHORITY and explicitly amends D-W5-15 to govern
  MEASURED counts only. An unpaired absence-only check is also the "unpaired-zero gate" anti-pattern —
  it reads green on a file where the wanted text was deleted.
- **Files modified:** none beyond the planned config.
