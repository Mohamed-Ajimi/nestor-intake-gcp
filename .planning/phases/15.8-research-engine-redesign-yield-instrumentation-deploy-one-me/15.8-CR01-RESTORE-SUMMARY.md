# Phase 15.8 — CR-01 Restore: the two yield columns are measurements again

`assignment_yield.fact_list_parsed` and `.resolvable_sources` were bound `None` by the review-gate
fix, because the only honest per-provider data lives upstream of `pipeline.py`. The operator
authorised touching `pipeline/synthesis/steps.py`. Both columns now carry real per-assignment
measurements, and **all eleven `assignment_yield` columns are measured** going into the one ~$45 run.

Engine gate: **`collecting: 43 of 43` · 1777 passed · 0 failed · 13 skipped**, build
`409ecddc-96ac-4862-8e2b-765b7ffa2509`, STATUS read from `gcloud builds describe`.

---

## THE ONE THING TO READ IF YOU READ NOTHING ELSE

**The repair does not carry a claim list out. It carries an AGGREGATE, and that distinction is the
whole fix.**

Both traps the previous attempt hit are real and were re-confirmed here rather than trusted:

1. `_dedupe_claims` **mutates the surviving dict in place**, so any snapshot of claims — shallow or
   otherwise — shows the merged `found_by` and the unioned `source_urls` afterwards.
2. The merge **already ran** inside `collect_provider_facts` before `pipeline.py` binds a claim.

A deep copy would have solved (1) and not (2), and would have meant copying every claim in a paid
run to compute two integers. Instead `collect_provider_facts` measures each assignment **inside its
report loop**, at the one moment the answer exists, and what leaves that loop is a `bool` and a set
of URL **strings**. Nothing downstream holds a claim, so there is nothing left for the in-place merge
to reach. The trap is not avoided — it is unreachable.

Those values ride out on the `reports` entries as two `_`-prefixed keys, exactly as the seven other
per-assignment values already do, and `_assignment_yield_rows` reads them with `.get()`.

## What changed

### `pipeline/synthesis/steps.py` — STRICTLY ADDITIVE (zero deleted lines, verified mechanically)

- Two exported constants, `ANGLE_YIELD_FACT_LIST_PARSED` / `ANGLE_YIELD_RESOLVABLE_SOURCES`.
  **Imported as symbols by `pipeline.py`, not matched as string literals** — the seven sibling keys
  are matched by bare literals in two modules, which is the agree-by-accident shape this phase spent
  three plans closing. A rename is now an ImportError instead of a silently NULL column.
- `_collect_claim_urls(sink, claim)` — copies a claim's URLs out **as strings**, using
  `_assignment_yield_rows`' original normalisation verbatim (distinct, stripped, non-empty,
  string-only) so the column means what it meant before CR-01 removed it.
- `_stamp_pre_merge_yield(reports_out, entry_yields)` — **ONE stamp site with an alignment guard.**
  The loop appends to `reports_out` at four different places; a probe dict is appended to
  `entry_yields` as the **first statement of every iteration**, so lockstep is a property of the code
  shape rather than something to remember. If the two lists ever disagree it **declines to stamp**
  and logs — a misattributed measurement is worse than an absent one.
- `collect_provider_facts`: four branches now record what they learned. Nothing else changed — not a
  claim, count, note, feed row, record or LLM call.
- `ProviderFactsResult` gains a **contract (e)** that extends (b) and states why the two added keys
  leave the drop-in-replacement property intact. The three existing contracts are untouched verbatim.

### `pipeline/tribunal/pipeline.py` — two bindings and a docstring

The only executable deletions in the whole file are `fact_list_parsed = None` and
`resolvable_sources = None`. `claims_kept` **does not appear in the diff at all**.

### The six shapes, stated once

| what happened to the report | `fact_list_parsed` | `resolvable_sources` |
|---|---|---|
| forced-tool facts (own-researcher) | `True` | count (0 possible) |
| fact list parsed first time | `True` | count (0 possible) |
| D-R2 corrective re-ask parsed | `True` | count (0 possible) |
| fell back to the distiller | `False` | `NULL` |
| no report text and no facts | `NULL` | `NULL` |
| raised before its list was read | `NULL` | `NULL` |

**`False` + `NULL` is the correct fallback row, not a hole.** A fallen-back angle's claims come out
of the ONE shared full-extraction distiller call, which has already discarded the angle — it passes
`corroboration_key=None` for exactly the same stated reason ("passing some other report's loop
variable would be a FABRICATED attribution"). A `0` would say the angle cited nothing; the truth is
that nobody can say what it cited.

### The semantics CHANGED with the source, and that is an improvement worth naming

`fact_list_parsed` is no longer inferred from claim `fact_source`. It is the **real per-report parse
outcome** — which the pre-CR-01 docstring explicitly wished for and said it could not have from
`pipeline.py`. Two consequences:

- a fell-back stream reads `False` **even when a corroborating stream's D8 block parsed** (that was
  the contamination);
- it is no longer `None` merely because the assignment kept no claims. "The list parsed and yielded
  nothing" and "there was no list" are different facts, and the column can finally tell them apart.

## Deviations and scope notes

**1. Two planning documents were corrected, and they were not named in the brief.**
`15.8-15-PLAN.md` and `15.8-UAT.md` both instructed the operator to record the two columns as NOT
MEASURED and **not to quote them**. Left alone, the one measuring run would have had its two most
D-R8-relevant columns deliberately ignored at readout — the change would have been inert. Both are
superseded **in place, marking rather than deleting** (the phase's own convention, and the original
text is struck through and kept). Each now also carries the two reader-side rules that did not exist
before: exclude fallback rows from a `resolvable_sources` comparison, and `FALSE` + `NULL` is the
correct fallback shape rather than a defect.

**2. `resolvable_sources` is NULL for a fallen-back angle, where a `0` was arguable.** Flagged rather
than silently chosen. A `0` is a positive claim that the angle cited nothing; the distiller path
genuinely destroys the attribution, so `NULL` is the honest record under this table's own doctrine.
The cost is that a naive `AVG(resolvable_sources)` per provider skews toward streams that parse —
which is why the exclusion rule is now written into both operator documents.

**3. The two constants are exported and imported rather than matched as literals.** The seven sibling
`_`-prefixed keys use bare literals in two modules. That convention was not followed here, and this
is a deliberate local deviation, not an oversight — a hand-copied key is the failure this phase has
closed three times.

**Nothing else was touched.** `_dedupe_claims`, the report writer, the verification stage,
`claims_kept`, the `assignment_yield` column set, `cloudbuild.test-engine.yaml`,
`cloudbuild.test-gates.yaml`, `STATE.md` and `ROADMAP.md` are all unmodified. No architectural
change. No checkpoint was required: nothing in the design touched a fenced surface.
