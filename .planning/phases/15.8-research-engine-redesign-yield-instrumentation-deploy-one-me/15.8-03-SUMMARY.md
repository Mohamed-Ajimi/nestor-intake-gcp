# Phase 15.8 Plan 03: CR-01 ×2 + D7 langs after the coverage repair — Summary

Closed the last two CR-01 stale-rank normalisation sites in `workshop_rank.py` by joining
both rank maps through one module-local `_text_key` on BOTH sides at the comparison site, and
stopped D7 `langs` being silently dropped for a winner minted by either of
`enforce_group_coverage`'s repair rungs by adding a second `_sweep_langs` call below the guard.

double evaluation.** The natural single-evaluation form is `key = _text_key(...)` followed by

future reader who collapses it back to the local has not broken anything, and the tests, not the
grep, are what hold the fix.

uncalled non-stdlib leaf the plan permits stubbing. Per the plan's own instruction I stopped rather
than growing a harness to fit. Substituted a STATIC placement check (arithmetic over the committed
source, labelled as such — it is not a behaviour result).
