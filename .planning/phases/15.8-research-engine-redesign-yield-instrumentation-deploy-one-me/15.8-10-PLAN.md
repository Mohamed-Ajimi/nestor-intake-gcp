## The critique triple is derivable at the site with no new computation of substance

`critique_candidates(candidates=population, ..., killed_out=killed_out)` returns `screened`.
Every entry in `screened` carries `critique` — `KEEP` or `WEAK`, never `KILL` (a KILL is
removed). Both coverage guards put a rescued candidate back into `screened` as `KEEP` **and
reconcile it out of `killed_out`**, so:

> `len(screened) + len(killed_out) == len(population) == candidates_in`, exactly.

The file's own idiom for reading the verdict, used verbatim one screen above the site, is
`str(entry.get("critique") or "").upper()`. Use that; do not invent a second reader.

**THE TWO DENOMINATORS, and why this plan chose the one it chose.** `round_metrics` keeps
`winners` (`len(selected)`) and `weak_winners` (WEAK among winners, cross-cutting-exempt, from
`exit_verdict`) **unchanged** — existing tests and `_stage_b_result`'s `loop_rounds` read them.
Those two are **WINNER-scoped**. The three new counters are **CRITIQUE-scoped**, over the whole
population the critique pass saw.

The columns take the CRITIQUE-scoped triple, because `ENGINE-REDESIGN-SPEC` § 6 asks for
*"KEEP/WEAK/KILL counts"* — that is the critique verdict vocabulary (`_CRITIQUE_ALLOWED`), not
a winner statistic — and because **a KILL cannot be winner-scoped at all**: a killed candidate
is removed before ranking, so there is no such thing as a killed winner. A triple whose third
member has no winner-scoped meaning must be critique-scoped in all three members, or it is
three different denominators wearing one name. That is precisely the mis-measurement D-W5-11
refuses for `winners`/`keep_count`, one column over.

**WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT PERSISTED, named so 15.8-15 does not discover it while reading the
table:** `winners`, `weak_winners`, `barred` (bars CREATED this round — a different quantity
from barred-duplicate DROPS), `lookups` and `calls`. D-W5-1's column set has no home for them,
and this plan does not own that schema. All five survive per-run in `_stage_b_result`'s
`loop_rounds`. **The consequence, stated once and handed forward: WEAK-winners-per-round is not
cross-run queryable after this phase.** That is a known limit of D-W5-1's column set, not an
