### DEFECT (1), fixed with a red-before/green-after proof

**`test_pipeline_assignment_yield.py::test_every_row_carries_exactly_the_emitter_keyword_set`**

Asserted `set(rows[0]) == _FIELDS`, where `_FIELDS` is a literal **in the same file**.
Its own docstring says a failure means `record_assignment_safe` raises `TypeError` on
`**` unpacking — but the emitter was never consulted. Both sides moved together, so the
assertion stayed green **exactly when `record_assignment` had drifted underneath it**.

The two sides are owned by **different plans in different worktrees**: the row builder
(`_assignment_yield_rows`, `pipeline.py`) is 15.8-09's; the emitter (`record_assignment`,
`runs/yield_records.py`) is 15.8-05's. They agree today by construction, not by assertion.

Fix: `_emitter_keywords()` reads `record_assignment`'s `KEYWORD_ONLY` parameters via
`inspect.signature`; the literal is kept as a pinned third party so a disagreement is
attributable rather than merely visible.

The OLD column staying green on every drift **is** the defect.
