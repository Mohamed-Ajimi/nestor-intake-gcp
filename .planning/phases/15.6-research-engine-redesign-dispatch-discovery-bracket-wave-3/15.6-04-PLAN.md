## PENDING OPERATOR RULING - read before Task 2

**The question.** May a group span two client questions?

**Why it is open.** Spec section 4 / D-R4 says explicitly that a group *"may span two client
questions where they overlap"*, and names that as where the saving comes from. But verification
against the merged tree on 2026-07-29 established that a mixed group **mis-attributes every one of
its claims**: on the primary D8 fact-list path `facet` is stamped in Python from the angle
(`steps.py:2400`), the D8 contract has **no facet column** (`facts.py:298`), and the model-supplied
FACET at `steps.py:1627` is the D-14 fallback path only. So a mixed group hands the top-ranked
member's parent to every claim it produces, including the ones answering the other client question -
and nothing downstream corrects it. `facet` becomes a confident lie exactly when grouping does the
thing it exists to do.

**The planner's recommendation: PREFER SINGLE-PARENT, ALLOW MIXED ONLY WHEN THE CEILING FORCES IT.**
- At <= 5 client questions (V-01 had 3) every group is single-parent, `facet` is exact by
  construction, and **the paid-call count is unchanged** - 3 questions plus discovery still costs
  4 x 3 + 3 = 15, the same as the mixed-group plan. The cost is duplicated groundwork INSIDE a call,
  not an extra billed call.
- At > 5 client questions single-parent grouping is arithmetically impossible under D-W3-1's hard
  ceiling of 5, so mixed groups are forced. There, the imprecision is flagged (`mixed_parents`),
  warned about per group (plan 15.6-03(f)) and measurable at the 15.8 run.
- The within-question saving section 4 actually describes - *"shared groundwork is searched once
  instead of five times"* - is fully preserved; it is the CROSS-question merge that is restricted.

**Rejected alternatives, with reasons, so the operator can overrule on the merits:**
- *Add a facet column to the D8 contract.* The correct long-term fix, and the only one that gives
  `resolved_facet` a genuine caller. Rejected NOW as high-risk: it changes a provider format contract
  that has already drifted twice in production (`facts.py:159` - `STATEMENT` written as a leading
  column on all four lines of one report; `facts.py:143` - `[cite: 25, 26]` in the SOURCE_URL column
  of all twenty lines of another), and `gate_errors: 153` on V-01 was that same class. Candidate for
  15.7 / 15.8 once the yield data justifies it.
- *Deterministic post-hoc matching of each fact to a member in Python.* This is fuzzy matching by
  another name, and this repo has the scar: V-01's exact-string corroboration key produced 396 keys
  for 396 claims, and only 37 of 396 claims had ANY cross-provider partner at Jaccard 0.2.
  `resolved_facet`'s own docstring warns against turning its exact lookup fuzzy. A confidently wrong
  Python match is worse than a known-imprecise default because it looks principled.
- *Accept and record only.* Everything in that option is already being done by plan 15.6-03 - but on
  its own it leaves the primary path routinely mis-attributing, which is the failure the phase
  boundary says grouping must not cause.

**Until the ruling lands, use the recommendation** (`prefer_single_parent = True`,
`GROUP_RULE_SINGLE_PARENT_ONLY`). If the operator rules the other way, this plan changes by exactly
two constants and no code. **Do not change plan 15.6-01's clamp - it implements both and is
neutral.** State in the SUMMARY which value you used and whether a ruling had arrived.
</objective>
