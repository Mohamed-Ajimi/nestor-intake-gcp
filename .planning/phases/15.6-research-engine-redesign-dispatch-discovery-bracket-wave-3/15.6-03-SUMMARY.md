## What Was Built

**`_divide_from_winners` stops dealing.** Steps 1 (normalise and bound) and 4 (trim) are unchanged; steps 2 and 3 replaced the deal:

- **Resolve.** A caller that already grouped the winners passes them in. When it did not, the winners are grouped one group per client question through `question_grouping.fallback_groups` — the single implementation, whose other call site lives inside `group_winners` — and a `log.warning` says so in words.
- **Dispatch.** Every surviving group goes to every entry in `_D6_STREAMS`. No top-k, no remainder, no round robin, no per-angle provider preference. Uniform allocation is the honest choice because there is no trustworthy yield data to route on; 15.8 collects it.

**Every angle is therefore a corroboration copy, and the group IS the key.** `corroboration` is always `True` and `corroboration_key` is always the group id. That is the D-R4 payoff the CONTEXT promised: the column that was NULL for ~12 of 15 winners now populates for every claim, and 15.5 was right not to fake it.

**Two conditional keys whose ABSENCE is the signal.**

- `sub_question` is present only on a ONE-member group. On a multi-member group the key is **omitted entirely** — not `None`, not a join, not `members[0]`. `run_angles` reads it as `angle.get("sub_question") or None`, so absent means NULL, which is the true fact: the group answers no single sub-question. `sub_questions` carries the full ordered set, so nothing is lost.
- `mixed_parents` appears only when `len(client_parents) > 1`. It is **not** `len(parents) > 1`, and that distinction is the whole of D-W3-5.2.

**The warning does not cry wolf.** A group spanning two CLIENT questions is flagged on the angle and warned about once, naming both labels, the surviving `focus_area` and the consequence in plain words. A group holding one client question plus a discovery rider — the intended shape — produces no warning at all.

**The known imprecision is written where it is made.** A mixed group's `focus_area` becomes the `facet` of every claim from that group on the primary D8 path, and the comment names the four things a wrong facet reaches (`_propagate_stakes`, the facet-scoped anchor ledger, `claims_per_facet`, the `claim.facet` column) and the one thing it does not (`_one_section` still receives every provider report in full). It states explicitly that the distiller's seeded FACET is the `distiller_fallback` path and does **not** correct this.

**`_angle_query` takes a list, and the injection bound is per member.** One member reproduces the pre-15.6 query byte-for-byte (verified against the phase base over five inputs). From two members up it emits a plural framing sentence, one numbered line per member, then the plural ignore-instructions line, then the D7 language paragraph last. Each member is collapsed and truncated to `_SUBQ_CHARS` **individually**: a 4-member group carries exactly 4 x 600 authored characters, driven and asserted.

**Constants.** `_D6_TOP_K` and `NESTOR_TRIBUNAL_D6_TOP_K` are gone. `_D6_HIGH_RANKS = 3` takes over the one job that survived — the numeric "high stakes" boundary — and is deliberately not env-backed, so stakes (and therefore gate priority and the report) cannot drift in a phase already changing dispatch. `_D6_MIN_CORROBORATION` and `_STAKES_PROVIDER` are kept with their records corrected. `_MAX_ANGLES` keeps the value 28 and the F5 REVERSAL paragraph verbatim, with the arithmetic re-derived: 5 groups x 3 streams = 15, leaving 13 slots, and the fallback reaches 28 at **ten** client questions.

**`degraded_parallel.py` — comment only.** `ALL_PROVIDERS` stays `("gemini", "claude", "openai", "own")` and `MIN_SUCCESSES` stays 2. The `own` reachability is recorded as a deliberate Wave 3 boundary that must not be "fixed" as an oversight, naming the three importers and what 15.8 must know. Proved comment-only by whole-module `ast.unparse` equality against the phase base while the raw text differs.
