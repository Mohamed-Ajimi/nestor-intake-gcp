# Phase 15.6 Plan 04: Group Coverage + the Stage-B Wiring Summary

**An LLM now decides which of the client's questions are researched together, so Python re-asserts D4
over the groups after the model has spoken — counting mandate MEMBERS rather than mandate groups, so a
question the evidence raised can never stand in for one the client asked.**

## What Was Built

**`enforce_group_coverage` (Task 1).** A sibling of `enforce_scope_guard`, one level up. It reuses that
function's repair ladder verbatim in substance — promote the missing label's best-ranked unused
candidate, else inject the client's own wording — and places every repair at the HEAD of both the
winners list and the group list, then re-ranks densely from 1. The placement is the load-bearing part:
`research_division._stakes_for_rank` derives stakes and stream treatment from `rank`, so a repaired
client question at the bottom of the list would receive the weakest treatment in the run.

**The coverage rule counts MANDATE MEMBERS.** Two exclusions, and they are different things: the whole
`d1` cross-cutting group is skipped because its members are parented `__discovery__`, and **a discovery
RIDER inside a mandate group is skipped too**. That second one is what D-W3-5 forces. A rider parented
`"Q2"` sits inside Q2's own group, so a group-level rule would read Q2 as covered even after Q2's own
winners had all been dropped. Driven directly, with the negative half asserted in the same test: the
group really does carry `"Q2"` in `parents`, so the group-level rule really would have passed.

**The ceiling ladder, and its precedence.** Room in the mandate's allowance → add the group. Otherwise
drop the cross-cutting `d1` group and take its slot, returning its members through `shed_out` so the
caller reports them as raised-but-not-researched — D-W3-4 says nothing in the mandate may be displaced
by a discovered question, so the displacement runs the other way. Otherwise exceed the ceiling, with an
ERROR naming both rules, because D4 coverage is a **scope invariant** and D-W3-1's five is a **spend
dial**. All three rungs are driven; rung 3 is documented as unreachable while the grouping partition is
total, and pinned anyway so a future edit fails loudly rather than quietly shrinking the operator's
scope.

**Stage B now groups and discovers (Task 2).** `allocate_discovery` over stage A's `brief_conflicts`,
then `partition_discovery`, then the grouping call, then rider attachment, then the `d1` group, then the
coverage guard, then a discovery-rank re-stamp. `groups`, `discovery` and `discovery_not_researched`
join the published contract on **every** path including the crash path, and `counts` gains seven keys.

**The seam's fate is now a written decision (Task 3)** — see the section below — and
`test_workshop_scope_guard.py` gained 23 tests and a 32-mutation battery.

## SEAM: LEFT UNCALLED, AND WHY

**Branch taken: LEFT UNCALLED.** The plan asked me to verify the CONTEXT's claim that this phase is the
facet-resolution seam's first production caller. It is not, and the evidence is stronger than the plan
anticipated.

**What I could and could not read.** Plan 15.6-03 owns `research_division.py` and is running in my wave,
so its `_group_angle` is **not visible to me** — no merged tree containing it exists yet. That is stated
plainly rather than papered over. What I did read, at the phase base, is the code the argument actually
turns on:

| Read | File | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| `_angle` | `research_division.py:836-861` | `focus_area = w["parent"] if w["parent"] in parent_prompt else labels[0]`; `sub_question = w["text"]` |
| `build_mission_brief_from_winners` | `research_division.py:575-612` | `focus_areas` is built from the **client labels only**; an unmatched parent is attached to `labels[0]`, "rather than dropped" |

**The four residual cases, all written into the two docstrings:**

1. **A discovery RIDER files under its host client question.** Accepted by D-W3-5.2, and the resolver
   could not improve it — the rider's own `parent` IS that host label.
2. **A `d1` cross-cutting claim files under `labels[0]`.** Resolving would return `__discovery__`, which
   is not a client question and therefore not in `focus_areas`, so `_propagate_stakes` would silently
   default its stakes and `claims_per_facet` would gain a key with no report section behind it. **Worse,
   not better** — stated explicitly, because it is the obvious "improvement" the next reader attempts.
3. **THE FINDING THE PLAN DID NOT ANTICIPATE — the ORPHAN path is where the two values genuinely
   DIFFER, and resolving is still worse.** `_angle` sends a winner whose parent matches no
   client-validated label to `labels[0]`, while a text→parent index returns the unmatched label itself.
   Because `focus_areas` is built from client labels only, resolving to it would move the claim out of
   every report section and out of the client question's own count — exactly what the orphan rule exists
   to prevent. The plan told me to STOP and report `SEAM: CALLER FOUND` if I found a divergence; I found
   one, and it argues the opposite way, so it is recorded here as a fourth case rather than as a reason
   to wire the seam in. **If the operator disagrees with that reading, this is the sentence to
   challenge.**
4. **More than five client questions**, where the ≤5 ceiling makes single-parent impossible and one
   group may legitimately span two. The only case with anything to gain, and still unreachable: a
   per-claim resolution needs a per-claim sub-question, one group angle covers the whole group, and
   phase 15.5 ruled that fabricating a sub-question is worse than a NULL.

**The one condition that would make it a caller:** a genuine per-claim sub-question attribution
channel, which needs a `facet` column in the D8 fact-list contract. Out of scope, and high-risk for the
reasons D-W3-5 records.

**Where the explanation lives, and why that matters.** All of it is in `claim_attribution.py`'s two

## Deviations from Plan

### 1. [Rule 1 — Bug] `group_winners` has no `prefer_single_parent` parameter

- **Found during:** Task 2, reading the merged Wave 1 signature before binding to it.
- **Issue:** The plan pins the call as `group_winners(..., prefer_single_parent=_PREFER_SINGLE_PARENT,
  ...)` and instructs me to define `_PREFER_SINGLE_PARENT = True` in `workshop_rank.py`. The real
  signature (`question_grouping.py:1196`) has no such parameter — `group_winners` pins
  `prefer_single_parent=True` internally at `:1333` with the exact D-W3-5 comment the plan asked for,
  and derives its prompt sentence from `len(labels) <= ceiling` so the sentence and the clamp cannot
  disagree.
- **Resolution:** Did **not** add the constant. Adding it would create precisely the two-values-to-keep-
  in-step pair the plan's own rationale forbids ("one value, one call site") and the dead-knob trap
  D-W3-2 names about `_D6_TOP_K`. The D-W3-5 three-sentence explanation the plan wanted **is** written,
  as a comment at the single grouping call site, plus a paragraph in the module docstring.
- **Files:** `workshop_rank.py`
- **Commit:** `b14f58c`

### 5. [Rule 2 — Missing critical] The discovery ranks had to be re-stamped after the repair

- **Found during:** Task 2, tracing the coverage guard's effect on the winner count.
- **Issue:** The plan stamps discovery `rank` as `len(final) + i + 1` at step 1, before the coverage
  guard at step 5 can PREPEND repair winners. With 12 winners and two discovered questions at ranks 13
  and 14, one repair makes the winners 1..13 — and rank 13 now belongs to both a client winner and a
  discovered question. `_stakes_for_rank` would then hand a discovered question a client question's
  stakes, which is exactly what D-W3-4 forbids.
- **Fix:** `_stamp_discovery_ranks(groups, discovery, base=len(final))` runs after the guard and
  re-stamps both the dispatched list and the matching group members (group members are copies, so the
  two would otherwise drift). The step-1 stamp is kept as a *provisional* one, because distinct numbers
  are what make rider shedding deterministic by allocation order instead of a `_RANK_LAST` tie.
- **Verification:** `test_discovery_ranks_below_every_winner_after_the_repair_grew_the_list`, plus
  mutation **M25**.
- **Commit:** `b14f58c`

### 6. [Rule 2 — Missing critical] Stale member ranks inside the groups

- **Issue:** A group's members are copies taken before the repair, so after `_rerank` they hold stale
  numbers — and `rank` drives stakes. Nothing in the plan re-stamps them.
- **Fix:** `_restamp_groups` refreshes every mandate member's rank from the re-ranked winners (joined by
  `text`, first-wins — `index` is `-1` on every verbatim injection and is not unique), recomputes each
  group's `rank` as its minimum member rank, and re-stamps `group_id` densely from `g1` so the ids stay
  dense after a repair group is inserted at the head. `d1` keeps its id.
- **Verification:** mutations **M10** and **M11**.
- **Commit:** `f82dd63`

### 7. [Rule 2 — Missing critical] The grouping call's cost is under a different key

- **Issue:** The plan says "fold `group_stats` into `calls` and `cost` alongside `critique_stats` /
  `tourney_stats` / `evolve_stats`". Those three write `cost_usd` as a string; `group_winners` writes
  **`cost` as a `Decimal`** (`question_grouping.py:1290-1292`). Folding it the same way reads `None` and
  adds zero — every grouping call would be reported as free, and the budget governor is inert by
  decision, so that number is the only spend signal the run has.
- **Fix:** `cost = _add_cost(cost, group_stats.get("cost"))`, with a comment saying why the key differs.
- **Verification:** `test_the_grouping_call_is_counted_in_the_stage_actions_and_cost`, plus mutations
  **M27** and **M28**.
- **Commit:** `b14f58c`

### 8. [Rule 2 — Missing critical] GAP A's dropped questions were losable

- **Issue:** GAP A discards the cross-cutting questions. Left as the plan describes them they would
  vanish between `discovery` and the report.
- **Fix:** They go into `discovery_not_researched` and produce a plain-words NOTE
  (`_note_discovery_yielded_its_slot`) rather than a degradation — every client question is still
  researched in full, so demoting the run for holding the mandate line is the D-12 alarm fatigue this
  module warns against. The same treatment is applied to anything rung 2 drops.
- **Verification:** `test_gap_a_...` asserts the note is present AND that no degradation carries it.
- **Commit:** `b14f58c`

### 9. [Rule 3 — Blocking] The plan's crash-path trigger cannot reach the crash path

- **Issue:** Task 2's acceptance asks for "a fake that raises inside `critique_candidates`".
  `critique_candidates` degrades internally and never propagates, so that fake produces a perfectly

- **Resolution:** The test injects an unexpected internal failure instead
  (`monkeypatch.setattr(workshop_rank, "_stamp_discovery_ranks", explode)`), which is what actually
  reaches the `except`. Recorded rather than quietly substituted.
- **Files:** `test_workshop_scope_guard.py`
