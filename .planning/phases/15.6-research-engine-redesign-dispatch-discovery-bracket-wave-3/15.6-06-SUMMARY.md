## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in the frontmatter. The two that a later reader is most likely to want to re-litigate:

1. **`warn_if_over_ceiling` is NOT called from `pipeline.py`.** The plan's item (d) asked for it there. It is already called once, at the only place that knows both the final group count and the stream count (`research_division._divide_from_winners`, immediately after dispatch), and `question_grouping.warn_if_over_ceiling`'s own docstring says the stream count "belongs to the dispatcher". A second call would double-log the one alarm the 15.8 run reads as its cost signal. The must-have — *"a run whose paid-call count exceeds the 5 × 3 ceiling says so loudly in the log"* — is therefore **satisfied UPSTREAM**, and this was verified by driving `divide()` with six client questions and no groups: 18 angles, and the alarm sentence naming 18, 15 and the inert budget governor appears at WARNING.
2. **The dispatch test needed a SPLITTING grouping fake.** On a one-client-question brief the correct grouping answer is one group — which is exactly what the D-W3-2 fallback produces, so a one-group test cannot distinguish "dispatch honoured stage B" from "dispatch ignored it". Splitting one client question across two groups is explicitly legal per `EMIT_QUESTION_GROUPS_TOOL` and is a shape the fallback can never produce. Without this the plan's headline must-have would have had a green test proving nothing (mutations M1 and M10 would both have been blind).
