<objective>
Close D-23.1-07 and D-23.1-08, and bound the `brief` field (CONTEXT § 5). `23.1-CONTEXT.md`
§ 4 verified two non-idempotent paid paths on the Tribunal run API:

- **`GET /{run_id}/report-proposal`** (`runs/api.py:371`) — on a cache miss it calls
  `build_audited_client()` + `build_report_proposal(...)` and INSERTs an `Output`
  (`api.py:405-415`). A billable, side-effecting GET. Two concurrent reads = two paid calls.
- **`POST /{run_id}/answer`** (`runs/api.py:269`) — reads the run, refuses 409 if
  `status != "needs_input"`, THEN writes. Two concurrent callers both pass the check and both
  queue a new run. `idempotency_key=uuid.uuid4()` is freshly random per row and protects
  nothing.

Purpose: both are money. The second is also correctness — two replacement runs for one answer
forks the audit chain the EU AI Act Art. 12 trail depends on.

Output: the advisory lock `execute.py` already owns, reused by the proposal GET with a
re-read of the cache UNDER the lock; a conditional UPDATE admission ticket on the answer verb;
and a `max_length` on `brief`.

## WHY NOT MOVE THE GET TO A POST

D-23.1-07 already ruled on this: the URL and the on-demand generation stay. The shaping panel
depends on the current shape, and moving it is a breaking API change for a live UI that buys
nothing the lock does not. Do not re-litigate it.

## THE BRIEF BOUND — THE NUMBER, AND ITS DERIVATION

`runs/schemas.py:111` and `:126` declare `brief: str = Field(min_length=1)` — a floor and no
ceiling, on `CreateRunRequest` and `CreateCompareRequest`.

Analytic worst case for a real brief, from the producer side at `047dcfe`:
`backend/app/research/brief.py::assemble_brief` carries the FULL `context_pack_text`
verbatim and untruncated, and that text is a Claude generation capped at
`_CONTEXT_PACK_MAX_TOKENS = 8192` (`backend/app/ai/skills/context_pack.py:52`) — call it
~32 KB of characters. Questions are individually clamped to
`brief_input._QUESTION_MAX_CHARS = 400`, the decision block to `_DECISION_MAX_CHARS = 400`,
report values to `_REPORT_VALUE_MAX_CHARS = 400`. Even at forty questions the assembled brief
lands well under 60,000 characters.

**Set `max_length=1_000_000`.** That is ~17x the analytic worst case. The bound is a
denial-of-service guard on the request body, NOT a content policy — pick a number no real
brief can reach so it never becomes a silent truncation of somebody's research.

⚠ **CROSS-PHASE INTERACTION, do not break it:** ROADMAP Phase 24 D-RR-3 specifies a superadmin
steering note with **NO length cap and no truncation**, injected into the brief. A tight cap
here would 422 that feature later. 1 MB leaves it room. Note this in the field's description
so the Phase-24 executor finds it.

  2. The transaction (and its connection) is ALREADY held across the existing
