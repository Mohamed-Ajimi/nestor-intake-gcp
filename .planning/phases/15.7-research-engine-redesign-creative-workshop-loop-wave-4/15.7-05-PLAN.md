<objective>
Build the D-R10 admission gate: the cheap grounded lookup that lets evolve INVENT an angle in any
round, and decides whether it earns a slot.

The ruling stands — the loop must DISCOVER, not only sharpen — but the admission test as originally
specified INVERTS ITS OWN PURPOSE and had to change. Read as "is there a published answer to this?",
it rejected ALL FOUR invented angles in the harness run. Zero survived. Among the rejected was "what
minimum network density is required for algorithmic pricing to pay off" — exactly the strategic
question a mid-sized player weighing expansion needs, and exactly what D-R10 exists to admit. The rule
as written admits angles that are already documented (already known, low research value) and rejects
novel ones (nobody has published it, high research value). It is a novelty filter pointed backwards.

The corrected test: verify the PREMISE IS REAL. Do the named entities, markets, mechanisms and metrics
EXIST, and could desk research plausibly settle the question — not has someone already settled it.

"No source, no slot" is NOT weakened. It moves from "only orientation may originate an angle" to
"only evidence may admit one".

Purpose: without a real evidence gate, the grounded lookup is theatre. The harness's own bug is the
lesson — a looser check (`if not url`) admitted 2 of 3 angles carrying a LITERAL DASH as the URL,
which is truthy: the model "evidenced" its own angle by tautologically restating that its own
entities exist.
Output: one new module, a new emit tool beside the existing ones, tests in an already-registered file.
</objective>
