## 1. THE SYSTEMIC FINDING — read this before the defect list

**The verification technique used by all nine plans is structurally blind to the defect that broke
the build.**

The `ast`-lift harness supplies module globals into the exec namespace. Commit `86d13ed`
(`feat(15.7-05): classify_parent`) added the function and reported *"38 lifted tests green"* — the
harness had **manufactured `DISCOVERY_PARENT` and hidden the missing import**. The ~10 genuine
`classify_parent` tests import that name into the *test* module, which cannot rescue the module's
own namespace.

**Every "N lifted tests green" claim in this phase carries that caveat retroactively.** It was not
that the tests were weak; the instrument was lying.

**Second pattern, four independent instances: defects that only fire on LOOP-BORN candidates.**
CR-02, CR-03, CR-05 and WR-`cluster_key` all behave correctly for stage-A-shaped data and wrongly
for anything the loop produces. Every test built on stage-A fixtures passes. **This is the exact
seam-failure shape Wave 3 shipped two criticals through, and it recurred four more times.**

**Third: `NEVER RAISES` is a docstring, not a structure.** `classify_parent` documents it and
raises on its first executable line. `run_workshop_stage_b`'s contract rests on ~10 such promises.

## 2. CRITICAL

### CR-01 — `DISCOVERY_PARENT` used, never imported → the whole workshop collapses
`workshop_admission.py:792, 812, 888, 965`; imports at `:83-87`.

Found independently three ways: the pytest gate (11 `NameError`s), `rev-evolve` (AST-lifted proof),
`rev-rank` (propagation trace). Verified by orchestrator: the import block pulls
`_DISCOVERY_TEXT_CHARS`, `_norm`, `_norm_url` and stops.

`:888` `out: list[str] = [DISCOVERY_PARENT] * len(angle_texts)` is the **first executable line**,
above every internal guard, so the function's NEVER-RAISES contract is void. There is **no
try/except between `workshop_rank.py:4127` and the outer `try` at `:3856`**, so it lands in the
blanket `except` at `:4601` and discards **every round's critique, the whole tournament, the whole
ranked pool** → `_fallback_winners`.

- **Trigger is the designed happy path**: any round where one INVENT survives its grounded lookup.
- **The money is spent first** — all lookups and all rounds complete, then are discarded.
- **Not silent**: `degradation_reasons` carries `"NameError: name 'DISCOVERY_PARENT' is not defined"`
  and `workshop_fallback: True`. An operator sees a real sentence.

**Fix:** add `DISCOVERY_PARENT` to the `:83-87` import. **Then re-run the real gate — the lift
cannot see this class at all.**

### CR-02 — clustering runs before indices are stamped → SILENT DELETION of research questions
`workshop_rank.py:4160` (cluster) vs `:4183` (stamp); `workshop_evolve.py:845`; `workshop.py:2387-2392`, `:2440`.

**Verified by orchestrator.** `_stamp_candidate` sets `"index": -1`. `cluster_candidates` runs 23
lines *before* `_stamp_loop_candidates`. The bucket key is
`index = candidate.get("index", position)` — the key is **present and poisoned**, so the `position`
default never fires. Every loop-born candidate keys to `__singleton__:-1`, lands in one bucket, and
**only `members[0]` survives**.

*Scenario:* round 3's clustering call times out → `_cluster_block` returns `[-1] * n` → six
freshly-evolved candidates collapse to one, logged as *"6 candidates collapsed to 1 after
near-duplicate clustering"* and reported by `_reason_cluster_collapse` as a **legitimate**
near-duplicate merge. Five new research questions are gone, indistinguishable from correct
behaviour. Also fires partially on the normal path for every index the clustering model omits.

The invariant at `workshop.py:2326` (*"a negative cluster id is still the never-drop sentinel"*) is
**false for this caller**.

**Fix:** move `_stamp_loop_candidates` above `:4160`; add a `position` fallback in `workshop.py:2387`.

### CR-03 — an ADMITTED angle is never barred → re-bought and duplicated every round
`workshop_rank.py:4082-4089`, `:4110-4120`, `:3617`; `workshop_admission.py:672-684`.

Found independently by `rev-rank` and `rev-evolve`. Only **dropped** inventions are barred
(`BAR_LOOKUP_FAILED`). Admitted ones leave `new_candidates`, never join `population`, are never seen
by `cluster_candidates`, and never enter the register — so round 2's prompt has **no record the
angle exists**. `_fill_remaining_discovery_slots` has no text dedup (unlike `allocate_discovery`'s
Rule 5).

*Scenario:* round 2 invents "X", buys a grounded lookup, is admitted, takes discovery slot 1. Round
3 invents "X" again, buys a **second** paid lookup, is admitted again, appends a **second identical
entry**. Over 10 rounds all 5 discovery slots can hold the same question, dispatched as 5 separate
paid research questions.

This is **verbatim** the defect `workshop_register.py:136-140` cites as its reason for existing —
closed for the half that fails, left open for the half that costs money.

### CR-04 — `tournament_rounds` skips both ceilings on a positive override
`workshop_loop.py:184-188`. **Verified by orchestrator.**

`if explicit > 0: return explicit` returns before `min(_TOURNAMENT_ROUNDS_MAX, floor, total - 1)`.
Measured: `tournament_rounds(30, override=10**9)` → `1000000000`; `tournament_rounds(3, override=50)`
→ `50`. Every round is a real LLM batch, inside a loop running up to 10 times.

**Two in-tree statements are false because of this** — `workshop_loop.py:92-94` and
`workshop_rank.py:930-932`, the latter being the stated justification for `_CATCH_UP_MAX_MATCHES`,
labelled *"T-15.7-08-03, DENIAL OF WALLET"*. The catch-up half got its ceiling; the rounds half only
claims one. Realistic trigger is an operator typo.

**Fix:** `return max(0, min(_TOURNAMENT_ROUNDS_MAX, explicit, total - 1))`.

### CR-05 — `BAR_WEAK_TWICE` bars a candidate that stays live; the bar's only effect is blocking its repair
`workshop_register.py:44-46`, `:148` vs `workshop_rank.py:3988-3999`, `:4236`. **Verified by
orchestrator:** `population` has exactly one assignment in the loop — `population = population +
stamped`. It only ever grows; `select_winners` takes no register.

Chain: W is WEAK in rounds 1–2 → barred → **still in `population`**, still critiqued, still ranked,
still selectable. `_pick` falls back to `eligible[0]` when no KEEP is eligible, so W takes a floor
slot on any question with <5 KEEPs → `exit_verdict` counts a WEAK winner → `quality_ok` False every
round → **the loop runs the full 10-round cap**. Then round 3's sharpened version of W clusters onto
W's own barred shadow and is dropped, and `drop_summary` reports *"the loop is SPINNING"* — when the
bar caused it.

**The measured design and the implementation diverge:** `exit_verdict`'s docstring says barring
*"stripped every KEEP candidate from one client question"*. In the harness a bar removed candidates;
here it removes nothing. **The validated convergence behaviour is not the implemented behaviour.**

### CR-06 — `stats` overwritten, not accumulated → cost and calls report the final round only
`workshop_rank.py:2092-2095`, `:730-731`.

`run_tournament` and `critique_candidates` **assign** `stats["calls"]`; every sibling
(`workshop_evolve`, `workshop_admission`) accumulates. The loop reuses one `critique_stats` and one
`tourney_stats` across all rounds.

On a 9-round run, `calls` and `cost` count **~1/9** of the two most call-heavy stages.
`round_records[…]["calls"]` adds two per-round numbers to a cumulative one and means nothing.
`run_tournament` also early-returns without writing `stats`, leaving the previous round's numbers in
place.

**`round_metrics`' own docstring says the recorded number is the only thing that could ever justify
a ceiling — it is currently wrong in a way that still looks plausible.**

### CR-07 — the admission gate's spend never reaches the run totals
`workshop_rank.py:4516-4537`.

`admission_cost_usd`, `admission_calls`, `admission_resolver_calls`, `classify_calls` and
`cluster_stats["calls"]` are all produced and **none are read** by the rollup. The grounded
lookups — the loop's only web-grounded paid component, and the one T-15.7-08-03 names as the
denial-of-wallet risk — report **$0.00 and 0 calls**. Two-line fix.

**CR-06 + CR-07 together mean the instrumentation for the single 15.8 measuring run is wrong — and
that measurement is what 15.8 exists to produce.**

### CR-08 — the paid provider receives the client's question TRUNCATED AT 120 CHARS, mid-word, with the brief absent
`workshop.py:170`, `:678`; `research_division.py:794`, `:1214-1217`, `:1301`. **Verified by
orchestrator; driven against committed source by `rev-seams`.**

Four individually-defensible links:
1. `pipeline.py:1708-1711` passes `{"label": q, "text": q}` — the label **is** the full question.
2. `normalise_questions:678` stores `label[:_LABEL_MAX_CHARS]` — **120**. That constant is commented
   as *"a dict key and the join key"*; **nobody recorded that it is also what a provider reads.**
3. `build_mission_brief_from_winners:794` sets `"research_prompt": prompts.get(label) or label`, and
   **no caller anywhere passes `parent_prompts=`** (orchestrator-verified: the parameter is
   referenced only inside its own function body), so `prompts` is always empty →
   `research_prompt == label`.
4. Because `research_prompt` is non-empty, the `f"{label}: {base_prompt}"` fallback at `:1216` is
   **unreachable** — `deep_research_prompt` never enters a mandate angle. `_group_angle:1301` uses
   the fragment verbatim as the assignment.

Measured output ends mid-word at `"…gegeven de dalende brand"`. `_angle_query`'s docstring promises
*"the parent assignment comes FIRST and verbatim"* — the engine-authored assignment is a fragment.

**And the inversion:** the cross-cutting **discovery** group is the only group that receives the

**Predates 15.7** (this phase's diff to the file touches only `_D6_MAX_WINNERS`) — so it would have

### CR-09 — THE FOURTH CLIP: `_D6_MAX_GROUP_SIZE = 7` sheds every discovery rider at the validated config
`question_grouping.py:148-174`, `:1103-1126`. **Driven against committed source.**

The constant was raised 4 → 7 by plan 15.7-01 with the derivation *"7 = 5 winners + 2 riders"*.
**It forgets where the 2 cross-cutting winners go.** A cross-cutting *mandate* winner is not
`__discovery__`-parented — `workshop_evolve._stamp_candidate:838` sets `parent = parents[0]`, a real
client label — and `fallback_groups` (the primary path) buckets purely on `_own_parent`. So both
land **inside a client question's group**, which is therefore **7 before any rider**, and
`attach_discovery_riders`' `while len(members) > size_cap` sheds every rider it just attached:

```
groups:      [('g1', 7, 'Q1'), ('g2', 5, 'Q2'), ('g3', 5, 'Q3')]
after riders:[('g1', 7, 0),    ('g2', 6, 1),    ('g3', 6, 1)]
SHED: ['discovered A']
```

Per-parent discovery cap is 3, so all three riders on that question are shed. `= 4` shed them; `= 7`
still sheds them. **The number moved; the defect did not.**

It survived because the derivation was **restated against the new premise rather than re-checked
against the function that consumes it** — and because the shed note is unreadable (see
`workshop_notes[:4]` below).

**Fix:** derive from what a per-question group actually holds —
`floor + cross_cutting + per_parent_discovery_cap` = 5+2+3 = **10** — or give
`attach_discovery_riders` a rider budget instead of a total-size cap. Do **not** move cross-cutting
winners out of per-question buckets; that breaks `_covered_by_mandate_members`.

- **`_flatten` re-implemented in `workshop_register.py:185-207`** — byte-identical today, **no

- **`barred_block` keeps the OLDEST 24 bars and drops the NEWEST** (`:451`) — the rejects the model

- **`record_drop` silently coerces an unusable `round_no` to 0** and stores anyway, while refusing a

- **`catch_up_matches` counts newcomers in its own median** → on a small field the median hits 0 and
