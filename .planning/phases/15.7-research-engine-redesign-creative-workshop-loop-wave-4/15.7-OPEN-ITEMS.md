## RULED — do not relitigate

### D-R9 reaffirmed — the tournament stays
**Operator, 2026-07-29: *"we are not killing tournament".*** Pairwise Elo is **retained**. § 5's
"Fix the arithmetic" paragraph offers replacing it with a ranked-list-plus-run-off and calls that
*"better at our size"* — **that option is REJECTED**, and the paragraph still reads as an open choice on
its face. The Elo-1200.0 ties are fixed by **raising the rounds** so every candidate plays ≥5–6 matches.

Cost is a non-issue: the entire 4-round tournament measured **6 × gemini-flash, 8.4k in / 400 out,
~$0.00**. Six to eight rounds is a handful more flash calls.

**Consequence, free:** § 8's A/B control `NESTOR_TRIBUNAL_WORKSHOP_TOURNAMENT=false` (ranks by index,
zero LLM calls) stays meaningful. It is what proves the loop earned its cost, and there is **only ONE
measuring run** to spend.

### D-R10 — the loop must DISCOVER, not only sharpen
**Operator, 2026-07-29.** Full write-up in spec § 5.

**The gap:** as written, the loop **could not produce a new angle after round 1**. Orientation runs
**once** (the cost table says so); a discovery question needs a source and its only source is
orientation's `brief_conflicts`; the mandate bracket **keeps its scope lock**. So the pool of genuinely
new angles was frozen at round 1 — on V-01, **two conflicts, both about Q1** — and ten rounds bought
sharper wording of those two plus enriched rephrasings of the client's own questions. That is the
rephrase the operator did not want, and it was specified rather than accidental.

**The rule:** evolve **may invent** an angle in any round. A **cheap grounded lookup** then admits it
(a real source found → discovery candidate, carrying quote and URL exactly like an orientation-seeded
one) or **drops and logs it** (nothing found).

**"No source, no slot" is NOT weakened** — it moves from *"only orientation may originate an angle"* to
*"only evidence may admit one"*, which is what the rule was always for.

**Unchanged:** D-W3-4's allocation still bounds what is **dispatched** — ≤5 discovery slots, per-parent
cap 3, never borrows from the mandate. D-R10 widens where candidates may *come from*, not how many run.

**Raises the stakes on the rejected register:** with invention allowed every round, the barred list plus
the `cluster_candidates` semantic drop (workshop.py:1482) are what stop the loop re-proposing its own
rejects. Log every drop — *"round 2 proposed 3 questions already rejected in round 1"* is the signal the
loop is spinning rather than exploring.

### D-R11 — Elo carries; newcomers seed at the field median
**Operator, 2026-07-29.** Full write-up in spec § 5.

**The gap:** the tournament runs **once** today. In this loop `rank` sits **inside** the cycle, so it
runs up to **ten times** over a growing population — and nothing said what happens to a rating between
rounds. Both readings fail differently: **reset** discards eight rounds of accumulated judgement every
round; **carry at a fixed 1200** makes every newly evolved candidate enter *below* an incumbent field
that has had rounds to climb, so the genuinely novel angle is systematically lowest-rated exactly when
slots are allocated — **which would have cancelled D-R10.**

**The rule:** ratings **carry** across loop rounds; a new candidate enters at the **current field
median**, never 1200.

**REQUIRED TEST:** a strong newcomer introduced in a late round **can still reach the top N**. Without
it this is unfalsifiable and the failure is silent — the same shape as ranks 8/9/10 finishing at exactly
1200.0 and taking research slots by tie order.

**Reading note:** "every candidate plays ≥5–6 matches" now means **within one loop round**, since
ratings persist across them.

## OPEN — needs an operator ruling before planning

### 1. The exit rule probably never fires — the expensive one
Exit requires **all three** criteria, one being *"no `WEAK` question remains in the winner set"*.
**V-01 had 9 of 10 winners `WEAK`.** If that is typical the loop hits the **10-round cap every run** —
yet § 5 says *"the cap is a ceiling, not a target, and you will rarely reach it."* **Both cannot be
true.** Cost of guessing wrong: ~**$3 and +5–7 min on every run** rather than occasionally.

Note this now interacts with D-R10: a loop that genuinely discovers has a better claim on 10 rounds than
one that only rephrases. Decide after, not before, deciding how much discovery is expected.

### 2. Two unset numbers — decide together
- **The spend ceiling.** Named as the second backstop and never given a value. § 5 names the shape it
  must bound: *a round-9 prompt carrying 60 candidates*, which a round-count cap does not catch.
- **A per-round ceiling on grounded lookups** (new, from D-R10) so an evolve call that invents twenty
  angles cannot spend twenty lookups.

### 3. "Barred this run, kept for the next" describes storage that does not exist
Nothing persists workshop state across runs. Either that is a **new table** — a **fourth** unpaid
migration — or the phrase means something narrower. `WEAK`-after-two-evolve-passes is the case it
governs.

### 4. Enrichment has no findings source for a discovery question
Evolve is to be passed *"the orientation findings for the parent question"*. A cross-cutting discovery
question's parent is **`__discovery__`**, which has no findings. Same class of gap D-W3-5 closed for
dispatch. Sharper under D-R10, since invented angles now arrive every round.

## Traps this phase inherits

- **The `WEAK`-winner coverage lie.** § 5's own boxed warning: `_reason_critique_resurrected` and
  `_reason_critique_population` are **coverage fallbacks, not quality passes**. If a resurrected
  candidate counts as a `KEEP`, the loop exits believing it met the quality bar when critique rejected
  everything. **Mark resurrected candidates and exclude them from exit criterion 1.**
- **Losers must NEVER be barred** from re-proposal. Load-bearing: the coverage guard promotes
  below-the-cut candidates when a client question ends up with no winner. Bar losers and the repair path
  breaks.

  comment. **The seam is still uncalled after 15.6** — its unlock condition is a facet column in the D8
  fact-list contract, which is a 15.7/15.8 candidate at best.
