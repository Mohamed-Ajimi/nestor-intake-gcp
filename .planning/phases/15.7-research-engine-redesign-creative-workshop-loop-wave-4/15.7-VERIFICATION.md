## THE SHARPEST QUESTION — is D-W4-3 honestly delivered?

Asked directly because it is this phase's headline fix and the `.continue-here` handoff flags it as a
possible silent no-op.

**Verdict: YES, D-W4-3 is delivered at the validated configuration. The no-op regime exists but does
not contain it.** The docstrings overstate the guarantee, and that is the honest defect.

The arithmetic, worked out rather than assumed:

- `catch_up_matches` returns `0` — and `_catch_up_pairs` returns `[]` immediately (`:1302`) — only
  when the low median of the field's match counts is 0, i.e. when **newcomers are at least half the
  field**.
- The validated configuration: 3 client questions × 12 = **36 candidates**. `tournament_rounds(36)`
  = 6, so after round 1 every incumbent has `matches = 6`.
- `_NEW_PER_ROUND = 6` (`workshop_evolve.py:208`), and INVENT candidates are removed from the pool
  before stamping — so **at most 6 newcomers enter a field of ~36**.
- Round 2's sorted match counts are `[0]*6 + [6]*36`; the low median is `values[20] = 6`. Every
  newcomer gets a deficit of 6 and plays it. **The schedule fires.**
- The regime where it does not fire needs newcomers ≥ half the field — e.g. the review's `10@8 +
  10@0`. Reaching it requires the pool to have collapsed to roughly the size of one evolve batch,
  which the measured population range (23-41) never approaches.

So the headline fix works where it was measured. What is NOT true is `catch_up_matches`' and
`_catch_up_pairs`' unconditional phrasing ("a new candidate simply plays the matches it missed").
There is a regime in which it silently plays none, and nothing in the code says so.

**Recommendation:** do not reverse the two committed assertions. Add the boundary condition to the
docstring and, if anything, a `log.warning` when `median == 0` while some entry has `matches == 0` —
the failure would then be visible instead of silent. Recorded as an operator call, not fixed here.

**Adjacent, and worth an operator glance before the measuring run:** `_CATCH_UP_MAX_MATCHES = 120`
(`workshop_rank.py:987`) was added on judgement, not measurement, and never binds in the measured
config. At 6 newcomers × deficit 6 the schedule buys 36 matches, well inside it.

## Gaps Summary

**The phase goal is achieved structurally. Every artifact exists, is substantive, is wired, and
carries real data. The nine Criticals are genuinely closed and I confirmed each in source.**

What is not settled is whether the loop will actually loop when it matters. `exit_verdict`'s
saturation criterion is vacuously true in round 1, so a healthy KEEP-heavy brief — precisely the
output the raised generation count and prefer-KEEP were introduced to produce — satisfies all three
criteria at the end of round 1 and the loop breaks after a single pass. In that case no candidate is
loop-born, no COMBINE runs, no cross-question synthesis happens, the meta-review's guidance is never
used, no INVENT ever reaches the evidence gate, and the two cross-cutting winner slots are filled by
ordinary single-parent candidates. The generative evolve call and any admission calls of that round
are paid for and discarded, because the exit check sits below them.

The executor met this twice, documented it, and called it correct behaviour. It may well be the
right reading of criterion 3 as written. But 15.7-CONTEXT records exp11 as both "winner set clean
from round 1" and "exits round 4" — under this implementation those cannot both be true. **I am
reporting the contradiction rather than resolving it.** It needs settling before 15.8's single
measuring run, because that run is the phase's only evidence and it could otherwise measure a loop
that ran once.

The other three gaps are narrower: D7 `langs` is dropped on the group-coverage repair path (a defect
the code review did not find, inherited from 15.6 but asserted unconditionally by this phase); half
the drop log has no production writer; and the barred list shows the oldest bars rather than the
newest.

**Nothing here blocks the build.** Two of the four gaps are one-line changes, and none of them makes
the tree worse than the pre-Wave-4 baseline.
