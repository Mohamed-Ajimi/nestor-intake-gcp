<objective>
Turn the workshop into the loop. This is the plan where every other plan in the phase meets.

`run_workshop_stage_b` today is a straight line: critique, tournament, cut, evolve, scope guard,
discovery, grouping. Wave 4 makes the middle of that a CYCLE — generate, critique, rank, select,
evolve, admit, meta-review, exit-check — up to ten rounds, with Elo carrying, a rejected register
living for the run, and three exit criteria that gate each other in turn.

**Read this before writing a line.** Wave 3 shipped 42/42 verification and 1283 green tests and still
had TWO CRITICALS — a prompt-injection channel and a silent deletion of client questions — because
each plan's `must_haves` were individually satisfied and the defects lived in the SEAMS BETWEEN PLANS.
This plan IS the seam. Its tests must assert behaviour END TO END through `run_workshop_stage_b`, not
through any single collaborator. A barred question that does not reappear according to the register,
but does reappear in the winner set, is exactly the shape of defect that got through last time.

Purpose: eight plans of parts become one loop, and the loop is judged by what comes out of stage B.
Output: `workshop_rank.run_workshop_stage_b` as a loop, with per-round instrumentation and the new
counts, and end-to-end seam tests.
</objective>
