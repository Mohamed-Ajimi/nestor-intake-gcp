<objective>
Make the tournament real (D-R6, D-R9) and make the newcomer rule actually deliver what it was ruled to
deliver (D-W4-3) — and close the half-built resurrection guard (D-W4-6).

Four things, all in `workshop_rank.py`, all in the tournament half:

1. **The judge is judging blind.** It sees two question texts, a short decision blurb and a
   160-character flaw clause. It emits literally `3 | A` — one letter, no reason. Give it the parent
   client question IN FULL plus the orientation findings for it, and make it say WHY in one clause.
   Three effects: better judgements, an audit trail of why 7 beat 9, and material for the meta-review.
2. **The arithmetic is short.** `_TOURNAMENT_ROUNDS = 4` over 17 candidates gives each candidate 3.76
   matches. The harness reproduced V-01's exact symptom — three candidates finishing at Elo exactly
   1200.00 with 2 wins each, straddling the top-10 cut, one losing its research slot to INDEX ORDER.
   Derive the round count from the candidate count so it cannot silently under-separate again.
3. **D-R11's median seed is INERT** — the standing sorts by `(-wins, -elo, index)` with wins primary,
   so median-seed and flat-1200 are byte-identical. D-W4-3 replaces it with a catch-up SCHEDULE, and
   the ranking code is not modified at all. Measured: 99.8 / 29.5 / 1.8 versus 1.5 / 1.5 / 0.0.
4. **Guard 2 does not mark what it rescues.** When critique kills everything,
   `_reason_critique_population` rewrites every candidate to KEEP UNMARKED — so the one case where
   quality most needs to read as FAILED reads as a perfect pass.

And one interaction that must be tested TOGETHER, never separately: **D-R9 makes D-R11's problem
worse.** More rounds give incumbents more matches and therefore more WINS, and wins is the primary
key. Measured, same newcomer, same rule: rank 6 at 4 rounds entering round 3; rank 11 at 8 rounds
entering round 6; rank 16 entering round 7 — a fail. The catch-up schedule is what makes raising the
rounds safe.

Output: `workshop_rank.py`'s tournament half rebuilt against `workshop_loop.py`'s pure arithmetic.
