land alone.)

I did **not** move cross-cutting winners out of per-question buckets, so
`_covered_by_mandate_members` is untouched.

### The derivation, rewritten to be re-checkable

The old comment was *restated against the new premise*; the new one states the rule and names the
function that must be read to falsify it:

- The shedding loop counts `_is_rider(member)` members **only**. Winners — cross-cutting or not —
  cannot crowd a rider out. **To re-check CR-09, read that loop and confirm it never tests
  `len(members)`.**
- `_D6_MAX_RIDERS_PER_GROUP = 3 = discovery_bracket._DISCOVERY_PER_PARENT_CAP`, the most riders that
  can legitimately reach one group, so a rider is shed only when discovery has already over-allocated
  against its own rule.
- **Where the cross-cutting winners live is now stated explicitly** in both the constant comment and
  the docstring: parented to a real client label by `workshop_evolve._stamp_candidate`
  (`parent = parents[0]`), bucketed by `fallback_groups` on `_own_parent`, therefore **inside a client
  question's group** — which is why that group is at 7 before any rider arrives.

`_D6_MAX_GROUP_SIZE` stays at 7 and keeps its separate `clamp_groups` job (splitting a proposed
grouping — rebalances winners, never deletes).
