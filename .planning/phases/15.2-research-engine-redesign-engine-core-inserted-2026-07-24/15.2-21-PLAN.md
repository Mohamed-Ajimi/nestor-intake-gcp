<objective>
**D-G + D-H — the headline defect. The workshop was fed the client's entire context pack as if it
were a list of research questions.**

Of the **32** "client questions" the workshop deepened on run `d6bb3aae`, only **11** were
externally-researchable market questions. The other 21 were the intake's own administrative fields:
`"Decision-maker: MOE, CEO"`, `"NDA status: intake says 'dont know'"`, and —
**six research sub-questions were generated for `"Output size (hard constraint): Standard (15-25
pages)"`**. Of the 11 paid deep-research angles that were actually dispatched, **3** were legitimate
external research. The client's flagship questions — dynamic pricing, competitor coffee strategies,
the supermarket-format threat — **were never dispatched at all**.

Compounding it (D-H), the workshop returned no research prompt, so the decision statement fell back
to the brief's opening line: **`Deep research for moetest.`** The tournament's entire job is ranking
questions by materiality *to a decision*. With no decision, ranking is arbitrary — which is exactly
why report metadata out-ranked the client's real questions.

**The root cause, exactly.** `backend/app/research/brief.py::assemble_brief` composes one string:
an opening line, an `Onderzoeksvragen:` header with the questions enumerated `1.`/`2.`/…, a prose
report hint, and the FULL context pack under a `[CONTEXT PACK]` header. The engine then throws that
structure away: `pipeline.py` calls `run_question_workshop(brief=brief, questions=None, …)`, and
`workshop.normalise_questions` falls through to `intake.detect_explicit_questions(brief)`, whose
`enum_re` matches `^\s*[-*•]\s+(.{10,})` — i.e. **every `- **Bold:** value` bullet in the context
pack markdown**, plus every line ending in `?`. The context pack template
(`backend/app/ai/prompts.py::CONTEXT_PACK_SKILL_PROMPT`) is built almost entirely of such bullets:
§3 the decision, §5 scope, §9 stakeholders and NDA, §10 output size and tone. 11 real + 21 bullets =
the 32 in the forensics. The funnel reconciles exactly.

**The operator's confirmed fix (2026-07-27, item 1):** the workshop takes ONLY the decomposed,
client-validated research questions. The context pack is passed as **context** — which is what it is
named for — and never as questions to be deepened, ranked or dispatched.

**Where the decision comes from once the fallback is unreachable** (D-H's open question, answered
here). The context pack's §3 *"De beslissing die eraan hangt"* opens with
`- **Wat moet beslist worden:** …` — that IS the client's decision, written by the context-pack skill
from the validated intake. The intake backend resolves it and emits it in a labelled `[DECISION]`
block; the engine reads that block. Precedence, most specific first: context-pack §3 → the intake's
own decision answer → the decomposition summary → nothing. "Nothing" is a **named degradation
reason**, never a silent substitution of the brief's first line.

**Split of work.** The engine gets a new pure module that reads the brief's structure; the intake
backend gets the `[DECISION]` block and its resolver. The engine's parser also recognises today's
already-deployed brief shape (`Onderzoeksvragen:` + `[CONTEXT PACK]`), so a run queued before the
backend redeploys is fixed too — which matters, because the whole point is that no further run
researches the wrong questions.

Purpose: this is the highest-value fix in the phase. Fixing the two dead provider streams (D-A/D-B)
would only have bought more expensive answers to the wrong questions.
Output: `brief_input.parse_brief`, the Stage-1 rewiring, `derive_decision_statement` + the
`[DECISION]` block, and two test modules — one per side of the seam.
</objective>

Recognised structure, in this order:

  - **The decision block.** Lines strictly between a line equal to `[DECISION]` and a line equal to
    `[END DECISION]` (or, when the end marker is absent, until the next line that is a recognised
    section marker or a blank line followed by a non-indented line). Joined, stripped, bounded to

    digits.

    allowed to READ and is never a question.
