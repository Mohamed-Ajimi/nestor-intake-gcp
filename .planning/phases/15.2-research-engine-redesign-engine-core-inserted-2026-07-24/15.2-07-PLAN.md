<decisions>
Four design decisions this plan settles. Record all four verbatim in the SUMMARY — the operator has
not seen D-07-A or D-07-C.

**D-07-A (F7 — re-entry routes through the group skeptic).** `EMIT_VERDICT_TOOL`'s enum is
`["support","refute","insufficient"]` and `tools.py:99-103` forbids extending it. D-11 resurrects
re-entry, so that asymmetry now has production consequences: a re-entered claim that is
true-but-overtaken would come back `insufficient` (survives, no caveat) instead of `superseded`
(G-06/G-07 caveat reaches synthesis via `contested_notes`). Re-entry therefore calls
`run_group_skeptic` with a **single-member group** — the cheapest correct move, per RESEARCH F7 and
PATTERNS. Rejected: extending the per-claim enum (explicitly forbidden in-source); leaving re-entry
on `_one_skeptic` (silently loses the fourth verdict on exactly the claims that got a second chance).

**D-07-B (the WR-01 cost trap).** `check_coverage` gains `selected_only: bool = True`, intersecting
the coverage surface with `(claim.get("gate") or {}).get("strict") == "VERIFY"` — RESEARCH's preferred
option over an inline filter at the call site, because it puts the rule in one auditable place and
makes the module docstring honest (it still claims high-stakes claims are the coverage surface, which
G-02 superseded). This **extends D-11, it does not contradict it**: coverage still means "a claim the
gates said was worth checking and that got no verdict gets one last chance", and bucket 3 and the
coverage surface now count the same population by construction.

**D-07-C (the breaker gate is `state == "closed"`, not `allow()`).** `allow()` consumes the single
half-open probe and would authorise a whole **fan-out** of N sessions on the strength of one probe
token. Re-entry proceeds only from a fully closed circuit; `open` and `half_open` both block, and the
uncovered claims go to bucket 3 with a named reason. This is the conservative reading of D-11 ("a
tripped provider means no re-entry") and it costs nothing in truth-telling, because the loss is
counted and stated rather than hidden. G-11's "fail toward MORE checking" does not apply: the
alternative is not more checking, it is more hard-400s (776 in 55 seconds on 2026-07-22).

**D-07-D (one thorough session, not three shallow ones).** Re-entry ran `for _ in range(3)`
`_one_skeptic` calls per claim. It now runs **one** `run_group_skeptic` session at
`_GROUP_DEPTH["high"] == (6, 8, 5)` — six turns, eight searches, five fetches. This is the engine's
own stated economics ("stakes controls the DEPTH of that single session, NOT the number of sessions",
`pipeline.py:648-654`), it is what the grouped production path already does for every checked claim,
and `adjudicate`'s majority-independent rule already treats one refute-with-independent-citation as
authoritative (`pipeline.py:105-107`). Note the pre-existing `range(3)` did not even match
`_PER_CLAIM_SKEPTICS`, which is 2.
</decisions>
