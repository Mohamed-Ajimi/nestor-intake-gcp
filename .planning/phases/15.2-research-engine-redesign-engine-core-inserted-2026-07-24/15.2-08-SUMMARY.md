# Phase 15.2 Plan 08: Incidental Checking, Unresolved Anchors and Degradation Reasons — Summary

A claim the gates dropped but a skeptic actually checked is no longer published to the
operator as "never checkable": it is a fourth accounting line, subtracted from bucket 2
per reason, so every distilled claim still lands in exactly one bucket.

## The six things the plan asked to be recorded explicitly

**1. The storage decision — all seven keys live INSIDE `run.verification_summary`.**
RESEARCH offered "siblings *of* `funnel`", which is incoherent here:
`result["verification_summary"]` **is** `verification_report["funnel"]` — the same dict
object, as `pipeline.py` says in a comment. There is no sibling slot on
`run.verification_summary`; it *is* the funnel. So the seven keys
(`checked_incidentally` + its four reason keys, `unresolved_anchors`,
`degradation_reasons`) went inside the funnel, and `RECORDED_FUNNEL_COUNTS` was extended
**in the same commit** — `test_gate_selector.py` compares the two key sets for equality in
both directions, so they are locked together and cannot be changed separately.

**2. The recorded values, and why — six zeros and one `[]`, all facts, no estimates.**

- `checked_incidentally` and its four reason keys = **0**. The recorded run had **no gate
  stage at all** — the same fact that makes `gate_errors 0`. No claim was gate-DROPped or
  `SKIP_STABLE` at run time, so no claim *could* be checked incidentally: the definition
  requires a gate decision that the run never made. The blind selection experiment's
  KEEP/DROP classification is **retrospective** and was never applied to that run's
  grouping. The fixture books all 198 covered claim slots inside `selected_verify` (which
  is what makes `should_have_been_checked = 424 − 198` correct), and `recorded/GROUPS.md`
  carries per-group claim *counts* with truncated claim text — not ids joinable to the
  answer-key TSVs — so any non-zero figure would be an invention, not a derivation.
  Operator rule, verbatim: **"NO ESTIMATES — facts and correct calculations only."**
- `unresolved_anchors` = **0**, definitionally: the run predates D-05 entirely, so no
  `[[c:…]]` anchor was ever emitted and none could fail to resolve.
- `degradation_reasons` = **`[]`**: that run's degradation is recorded as
  `verification_degraded: True` plus prose in `recorded/REPORT.md` (776 Anthropic cap-400s
  in 55 seconds), never as a machine-readable list. `verification/report.py` **derives**
  the bucket-3 sentence at read time, so the operator surface still names it — synthesising
  a sentence into the fixture would fabricate a recorded artifact.

The recorded three-bucket numbers are unmoved: **198 / 738 / 226 / 1162**, pinned by
`test_recorded_run_reports_zero_incidental_checks` and by the reconciliation test.

**3. The `orphan_cite_markers` / `model_invented_numbers` non-fold — a choice, not an
oversight.** Only `unresolved_anchors` was folded into the funnel. The other two are
diagnostics of the **provider's** `[cite: N]` mechanism, not D-06's `[[c:…]]` anchor
mechanism; they stay exactly where 15.2-05 put them (siblings of `funnel` on
`verification_report`, siblings of `verification_summary` on the result dict). Two
mechanisms, two counts, never conflated — that is 15.2-05's own in-source rule and this
plan honoured it.

**4. The bucket-3 wording split.** The pipeline names only what `report.py` cannot see (a
lost stream, a workshop fallback, a blocked re-entry). `report.py::_degradation_reasons`
owns the **single derivation** of the bucket-3 sentence and prepends it at read time. That
is what lets the recorded run — whose funnel carries `[]` — still name its degradation to
the operator, and it is why exactly ONE wording of that sentence exists in the codebase.
**Later plans (11 / 12 / 14 / 16) call plan 07's `_note_degradation` and must NOT write a
bucket-3 reason**, or the operator is told the same shortfall twice in two dialects. This
is written into the comment above the `_build_funnel` degradation line, where a future
reader will meet it.

**5. The accepted UI consequence.** `VerificationReport.tsx` renders
`Object.entries(report.funnel)` as generic `stage: count` chips, so the seven new keys
appear there automatically — including `degradation_reasons`, which renders as one long
comma-joined chip. That is precisely why the reason strings are capped at 200 characters
and the list at 8. **No frontend change was made**: the phase outline permits exactly one
frontend line, owned by 15.2-09. A dedicated block for these keys is a later-phase concern.

**6. Live-key failures in the full suite: none.** 897 passed, 42 skipped, 0 failed. The 42
skips are the known, already-diagnosed testcontainers `"host" network_mode is incompatible
with port_bindings` failures documented in `cloudbuild.test.yaml`'s own header — treated as
NOT-PROVEN, never as passes.

## What changed

### Task 1 — producer side (commit `5278e90`)

`pipeline.py`, `group_skeptic.py`, `loader.py`, `test_gate_selector.py`.

- **`_count_incidental`** (module level, before `_build_funnel`). Selects a claim when the
  gates did NOT mark it `strict == "VERIFY"` **and** `verdicts_by_claim.get(id(c))` is
  truthy. Attributes it to exactly one reason key; `SKIP_STABLE` → `..._stable`, otherwise
  the gate's `reason` maps to `..._not_falsifiable` / `..._not_load_bearing`, and
  **anything else** (`BOTH`, `None`, an unrecognised string) → `..._both`, which is the
  gate's own catch-all, so an unattributable incidental check is never dropped. The flat
  `checked_incidentally` total is set **here**, as the sum of the four, so total and
  breakdown cannot disagree. Never raises: every read is a `.get` chain.
- **`_normalise_degradation_reasons`** — the ONE D-12 normaliser. Non-empty `str` only,
  stripped, truncated to 200, deduped first-seen, capped at 8, `log.warning` when either
  cap bites (Rule 6), `[]` for a non-list rather than a raise.
- **`_build_funnel`** gained `incidental`, `unresolved_anchors`, `degradation_reasons` —
  all keyword-only **with defaults**, so the zero-claim call site's source literal stays
  byte-identical. The four reason counts are clamped to `max(0, …)` and then allocated
  against `dropped + skipped_stable` in a fixed key order, so a count above bucket 2's
  population is truncated deterministically and stated in a full sentence.
- **The real call site** computes `incidental` immediately after the bucket-3
  ground-truth reconciliation and logs a full sentence when it is non-zero. It passes plan
  07's run-scoped `degradation_reasons` — read, never re-declared.
- **`_verify_closing_item`** appends `· N also checked incidentally (…their verdicts
  count)` to the shared `counts` string, so both the degraded and the healthy row carry it.
  No `status` change, no reordering.
- **`_write_final_report`** folds `unresolved_anchors` into the funnel **from the sibling
  key on the same payload**, not from the local variable a second time — the two copies are
  then mechanically identical and the payload cannot publish two disagreeing numbers. The
  `done` feed row gains the anchor clause when the count is non-zero.
- **Plan 07's top-level surface** now goes through the same normaliser:
  `"degradation_reasons": _normalise_degradation_reasons(v.get("degradation_reasons"))`.
- **WR-10 documented at both sites**, comment-only. `group_skeptic.py`'s diff is 7 added
  lines, every one of them a `#` comment; no executable line added, removed or changed.
- **`RECORDED_FUNNEL_COUNTS`** extended to 20 keys with a `# --- 15.2 additive keys ---`
  sub-block, per-key provenance, and a new invariant line. `build_verification_summary`
  now `copy.deepcopy`s: a shallow `dict()` would SHARE the `degradation_reasons` list with
  the module constant, so one caller appending to it would corrupt G-13's single source for
  the rest of the process.
- **`test_gate_selector.py`**: exactly one line changed, inside an assertion *message*
  ("the 13 contract keys" → "the funnel contract keys"). The assertion expression is
  untouched — it is the guard that keeps the two key sets locked.

### Task 2 — consumer side (commit `94e9cb5`)

`verification/report.py`, `runs/schemas.py`, `test_verification_buckets.py`.

- **`_INCIDENTAL_KEYS`** added below `_ACCOUNTING_KEYS`, positionally paired with
  `_NOT_CHECKABLE_KEYS` (the `zip` in `_accounting` depends on that order, and a test
  asserts the pairing). The trap is spelled out in full above the tuple.
- **`_accounting`** gained a fourth line. Each incidental count is read with
  `funnel.get(k, 0)`, clamped to `max(0, …)` and then to its own raw bucket-2 reason, so a
  malformed funnel cannot drive a reason negative. `checked_incidentally.total` is the sum
  of the four **clamped** values — not a read of the funnel's flat key — which makes the
  one-bucket invariant hold by construction even for a hand-built funnel. `not_checkable`'s
  four reasons are the raw values minus the matching incidental values, and its `total` is
  the sum of those reduced four, so an operator who adds up the printed reasons gets the
  printed total.
- **`_degradation_reasons`** — new sibling of `_degradation` (whose `def` line and body are
  untouched in the diff). Normalises the funnel's list again (a hand-built or legacy funnel
  never met the producer-side normaliser), then prepends the derived bucket-3 sentence.
  Documents in its docstring that a recovered retry and `cost_pending` are **never**
  reasons (D-12), and that it shapes words rather than deciding the run's status.
- **`shape_verification_report`** publishes `unresolved_anchors`,
  `unresolved_anchors_text` (None on a healthy run) and `degradation_reasons` as
  **top-level siblings** between `unverified_note` and `true_cost`. Nothing was added
  inside `unverified`.
- **`VerificationReport`** declares all three, following the CR-02 precedent rather than
  relying on `extra="allow"`. No `RunStatus` change (that is 15.2-09's, already landed).
- **14 new tests**, all named in the plan, all PASSED.
