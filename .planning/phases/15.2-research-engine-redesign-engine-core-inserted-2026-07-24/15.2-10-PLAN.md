   calls go through `audited.anthropic_messages`; the Gemini clustering call goes through
   `grouping._cluster_block`, which already routes through `audited.gemini_generate`.

   `verify_chain` green is a legal gate (EU AI Act Art. 12, deadline 2026-08-02).
3. **No agent frameworks, no new package.** `tribunal/requirements.txt` is not edited. Hand-written
   async loops only (`group_skeptic.py` is the template).
4. **Untrusted-model-output parsing (ASVS V5).** Every parser here: pre-fill the output,
   regex/prefix-extract, bounds-check against `n`, clamp to a safe default, ignore garbled lines,
   **never `json.loads` raw model text** (use `skeptic._coerce_json` on *tool input* only, which is
   already structured), **never raise**.
5. **Prompt-injection controls.** Every prompt in this module embeds context-pack text, question
   text or web-page-derived findings. Truncate everything embedded and address by index; carry the
   ignore-instructions line. This is a security control, not formatting
   (`test_gate_replay.py:76-84`).
6. **Fail loud, in words.** Every loss — a failed orientation session, a question with zero parsed
   candidates, a candidate-cap overflow — is a plain-words entry in `degradation_reasons` and a
   WARNING log. Never a silent green.
7. **Tenant isolation.** No new table, no new endpoint. `run_id` / `tenant_id` are threaded to every
   `audited.*` call and to the caller-supplied `StageFeed`; nothing here opens a DB session.
8. **No local Python/Docker.** Author by construction; every verify command runs in Cloud Build.
9. **`security_enforcement` ON** — see `<threat_model>`. Threat IDs use the plan-banded
   `T-15.2-10N` (plan 10 → the 100s band).
10. **Env-tunable parameters use the `NESTOR_TRIBUNAL_*` idiom** (`gates.py:76-81`,
    `grouping.py:91-100`). RESEARCH rates the workshop shape **MEDIUM confidence** (Co-Scientist

    change.
