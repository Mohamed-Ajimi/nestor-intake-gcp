## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** `pytest nestor_pulse_sdk/tests/<the plan's own file(s)> -x -q` (authored by construction; executed in Cloud Build).
- **After every plan wave:** `cloudbuild.test-engine.yaml` **plus** `cloudbuild.test-gates.yaml` (15.1 must stay unbroken).
- **Before `/gsd:verify-work`:** all six gates green, in order — engine → gates → critical → full suite (live-key failures triaged, not ignored) → backend → scope guard.
- **Max feedback latency:** ~120 s for the engine gate.
