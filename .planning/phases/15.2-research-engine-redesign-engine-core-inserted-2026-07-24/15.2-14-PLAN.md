## D-15 — say it out loud, because 15.2-18 executes the deletion

CONTEXT.md D-15 is explicit: **"The planner must not treat 'remove the old engine path' as 'delete
`claim_distiller`'."**

What V-03 (plan 15.2-18) deletes is the distiller being the **primary** claim source — the
"distiller-as-shredder" wiring where every provider's prose was shredded into claims regardless of
whether the provider gave a structured list. That wiring is unwired by 15.2-15 and deleted by
15.2-18.

`claim_distiller`'s **ability to extract claims from a provider's prose stays alive** as the
per-provider D-14 fallback path built in this plan, and **it keeps its own tests**:
`tests/test_claim_distiller.py` and `tests/test_distiller_coverage.py` **must be green through
V-03**. This plan's Task 2 changes the distiller **additively only** — one new keyword-only
parameter, default `False`, default-path prompt byte-identical — precisely so neither of those two
files ever needs editing.

## D-14 — the rationale, and the two rejected alternatives you must not reintroduce

These are third-party deep-research APIs following a **prompt instruction**, and the format
**cannot be forced**: structured-output mode is unavailable for these call paths — *"citations ⊗
structured-outputs = HTTP 400"*, recorded twice in `synthesis/steps.py` (**:576-577** and **:626**).
D8's safety-net distiller covers *"the list is incomplete"*; it does not cover *"there is no list"*.
Hence full-extraction fallback.

The provider's research **still reaches the merge**. `certainty` and provider-stated quality are
left **null** (the domain heuristic fills the tier downstream, per D-13's provider-stated-wins /
heuristic-fallback rule). The fallback is recorded **per provider** and surfaced in the feed and the
verification report. **It degrades one stream, never the run, and nothing paid-for is lost.**

**Rejected — do NOT reintroduce:**
1. **A corrective retry first.** An extra deep-research call is among the most expensive calls in
   the run — on Gemini it is a full re-run. No retry of a research call is issued anywhere in this
   plan.
2. **Dropping the stream.** That discards a paid call and weakens corroboration for every question
   that provider covered.
