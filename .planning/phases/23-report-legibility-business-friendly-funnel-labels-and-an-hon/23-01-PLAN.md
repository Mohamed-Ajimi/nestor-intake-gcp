<objective>

dict keys. Replace each with a business-friendly label plus an explanatory tooltip in en/nl/fr, and

fault.

Output: one pure module plus its unit tests, 37 new locale key paths per language, and a
label + tooltip render replacing the raw `{stage}` span in the funnel row.

## PLANNING CORRECTION #1 — THE FUNNEL HAS 18 NUMERIC KEYS, NOT THE 6 THE UAT LISTS

`22-UAT.md`'s F1 entry names six keys (`checked`, `should_have_been_checked`, `verify_sessions`,
`checked_incidentally`, `unresolved_anchors`, `selected_verify`). Those are the six the operator
happened to see on the run they opened. **The component renders EVERY numeric entry of
`report.funnel`** (`VerificationReport.tsx:378-383`, `Object.entries(report?.funnel ?? {})`), and the
engine writes eighteen numeric keys into that one flat dict:

- **nine gate-owned** — `gates.py:120-130` `_FUNNEL_KEYS`: `distilled`, `kept`, `dropped`,
  `not_falsifiable`, `not_load_bearing`, `both`, `selected_verify`, `skipped_stable`, `gate_errors`
- **nine pipeline-owned** — `pipeline.py:1034-1076`: `checked`, `should_have_been_checked`,
  `verify_sessions`, `checked_incidentally`, `checked_incidentally_not_falsifiable`,
  `checked_incidentally_not_load_bearing`, `checked_incidentally_both`,
  `checked_incidentally_stable`, `unresolved_anchors`

Labelling only the operator's six would leave twelve raw keys on screen and the finding would come
straight back at the next UAT. **All eighteen are in scope.** `verification_degraded` (bool) and
`degradation_reasons` (list) live in the same dict but are non-numeric and are already dropped
before render by the Phase 22 CR-01 fix — do not re-fix that, and do not label them.
