<objective>
Wire the D-04 / D-06 completion path: when the poll driver detects a `completed` Tribunal run, it
runs `verify_chain` as a hard gate, and — only if the chain verifies — fetches the pieces, builds the
zip, uploads it to GCS ONCE, and persists `chain_status="verified"` + `bundle_key`. A broken chain
records `chain_status="broken"` + `chain_broken_at` with NO bundle (complete-but-locked, EU AI Act
Art. 12 posture). All seam + GCS I/O happens with NO pooled DB connection held.

Purpose: this is the phase's core guard + materialization. It extends the load-bearing Phase-16 poll
driver WITHOUT regressing its three fixes (commit-before-schedule, row-id idempotency key, diagnostics).
Output: a pure bundle builder + the extended completion branch + unit and integration tests.
</objective>
