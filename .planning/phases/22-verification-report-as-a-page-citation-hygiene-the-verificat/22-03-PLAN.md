<objective>
Make a `[n]` marker previewable on hover, and stop the product telling the operator a retrieval date
is a publication date.

Purpose: D-22-3 rules that hovering shows title + date + quality tier only, with the click behaviour
unchanged. Separately — and this is a live factual misstatement, on screen, right now —
`citation.published` renders `"Published: {{date}}"` for a field that carries `source.fetched_at`.
`citations/numbering.py`'s own docstring requires that field be labelled "retrieved", never
"published", because presenting a proxy as a fact is what the operator's "NO ESTIMATES — facts only"
bar forbids.

This plan also lands EVERY new locale key the phase needs, in all three languages, so that later
plans consume keys that already exist and no two plans race on the same three JSON files.

Output: a `CitationMarker` component, and three locale files carrying the phase's full key set.
</objective>
